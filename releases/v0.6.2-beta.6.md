# Mira desktop 0.6.2-beta.6

This beta adds Back and Forward across chats and panels, brings the Work tab to life, and tidies the window's top-left corner.

## Highlights

- **Back and Forward.** Arrows beside the sidebar toggle (or Cmd+[ and Cmd+]) step back through the chats you opened and the panels you opened or closed: the right panel, its tabs, the terminal and the review. Each chat has its own URL, so a reload reopens the chat you were in.
- **The Work tab.** The Chat/Work switch now opens Work, where chats are grouped by what they deliver: a pull request, or a worktree branch before it has one. Items are sorted into Needs you, In progress, In review, Ready to merge and Done. Each item opens a page with its goal, chats and agents, diff, branch, PR checks and reviews. Start work makes an issue worktree and opens a chat for it.
- **Traffic lights stay level.** The macOS window buttons now stay level with the sidebar toggle after resizing, focus changes, fullscreen and appearance changes. Before, they dropped back to their default position.

## Changed

- New thread and Search moved from the top toolbar into the sidebar, next to Pull request and Scheduled.
- Every sidebar row's icon now sits on one line. The chevrons on project folders and the Recents, Settled and Archived headings are gone; click the heading to collapse or expand it.

## Testing notes

- All 214 frontend tests passed. TypeScript, the production build, module-size checks and the 400 kB bundle budget (399.6 kB) passed. The desktop app builds and passes clippy.
- Back and Forward were checked in the browser: switching chats, opening and closing the terminal, then stepping back to the earlier chat. The traffic-light position was checked in a dev build of the desktop app at launch and in a test window through resize, focus, appearance and fullscreen.
- Full packaged desktop interaction remains unverified.
